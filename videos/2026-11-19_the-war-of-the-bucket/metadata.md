# The War of the Bucket — 41.6 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps

Facts (verified): Battle of Zappolino, 15 November 1325, at sunset near the castle of Zappolino (Emilia-Romagna).
Ghibelline Modena (about 7,000: ~2,000 cavalry + 5,000 infantry) defeated Guelph Bologna (over 30,000 infantry + ~2,000-2,500
cavalry). About 2,000 killed or wounded (infobox: ~500 Modenese, ~2,500 Bolognese casualties). The Modenese pursued the
Bolognese to the city and, on the way home, took a wooden bucket from a well outside Porta San Felice as a trophy. The war came
from decades of border raids and reprisals; "the war was fought over the bucket" is legend (popularised by Tassoni's
mock-heroic poem "La secchia rapita", 1622). The original bucket is kept in Modena's Palazzo Comunale; a replica is in the
Torre Ghirlandina.

## Script (voice: MiniMax "Decent Young Man", speech-2.8-hd, 610 chars, 41.6 s)
Two Italian cities once went to war, and the winner's prize was a bucket.
In thirteen twenty-five, Modena and Bologna had been raiding each other for years.
Bologna marched out with over thirty thousand soldiers. Modena had about seven thousand.
They clashed at sunset, near a castle called Zappolino. And tiny Modena won.
Around two thousand men were killed or wounded.
The winners chased the losers all the way to Bologna's gates.
And there, at a well, they grabbed the city's wooden bucket.
Legend says the war started over that bucket. It didn't.
But seven hundred years later, Modena still has the bucket.
