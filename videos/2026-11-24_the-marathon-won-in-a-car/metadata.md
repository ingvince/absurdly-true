# He "Won" the 1904 Olympic Marathon… in a Car — 2026-11-24

## Facts (verified)
- 1904 Olympic marathon, St. Louis, 30 Aug 1904, 3:03 pm; heat in the low 90s °F, roads inches deep in dust, cars of coaches/doctors kicking up dust (Smithsonian).
- One official water stop: a roadside well at mile 12 of 24.85 miles (Smithsonian, Olympics.com).
- Fred Lorz (USA) got cramps at mile 9, rode ~11 miles in an accompanying car, got out and ran to the finish; hailed as winner, about to receive the trophy from Alice Roosevelt (President's daughter) when he was exposed; said it was a joke; banned for life, ban lifted, won Boston 1905 (Olympics.com; Smithsonian for the joke + Boston).
- Thomas Hicks (winner, 3:28:53) was given strychnine + egg whites, later with brandy, by his trainers (Smithsonian; strychnine = rat poison per CDC quote there).
- 32 starters, 14 finishers; Carvajal ate rotten apples, napped, finished 4th (Olympics.com).
