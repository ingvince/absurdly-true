# The Ship That Sank in 20 Minutes — 39.3 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps

Facts (verified): Vasa, Swedish warship built 1626-1628 for King Gustav II Adolf; 64 guns on two gun decks, ~500 carved
sculptures. Before launch a stability test (30 men running across the deck) was stopped because the ship heeled too much;
Admiral Klas Fleming let the launch go ahead. Maiden voyage 10 Aug 1628, Stockholm harbour: fired a salute, a gust of wind
heeled her, water entered the open lower gunports, she sank after ~1,300 m (about 20 minutes). Inquiry punished no one.
Salvaged 1961 (333 years later); Vasa Museum, Stockholm, is the most visited museum in Scandinavia.
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasa_(ship) · https://www.vasamuseet.se/en/explore/vasa-history/timeline
