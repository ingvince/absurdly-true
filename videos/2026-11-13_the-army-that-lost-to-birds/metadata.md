# The Army That Lost to Birds — 40.6 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps

Facts (verified): Emu War, Campion district, Western Australia, Nov-Dec 1932. Major G.P.W. Meredith (Royal Australian
Artillery), two Lewis guns, 10,000 rounds; ~20,000 emus. 4 Nov ambush at a dam: gun jammed after ~12 birds.
Gun on a truck could not keep up / aim. Troops withdrawn 8 Nov, back 13 Nov. Final report: 9,860 rounds, 986 kills
(10 rounds per kill). Meredith: emus "can face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks". Crop damage continued.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War

## Script (voice: MiniMax "Decent Young Man", speech-2.8-hd)
In nineteen thirty-two, Australia's army went to war. Against birds.
Twenty thousand emus were eating the wheat farms.
So the army sent soldiers with two machine guns.
The first shots? The emus just scattered.
An ambush at a dam. A thousand birds walked right in.
Then the gun jammed.
They put a gun on a truck. The emus outran it.
The major said they faced machine guns like tanks.
After six days, the army retreated.
In the end, they fired almost ten thousand bullets.
Ten for every dead emu.
The emus won.
